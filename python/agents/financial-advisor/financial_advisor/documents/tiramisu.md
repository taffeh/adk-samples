# Tiramisu

A classic no-bake Italian dessert of coffee-soaked sponge fingers layered with
a rich mascarpone cream and dusted with cocoa. Serves 8.

## Ingredients

- 300ml strong espresso or coffee, cooled
- 3 tbsp coffee liqueur or dark rum (optional)
- 3 large eggs, separated
- 100g caster sugar
- 500g mascarpone, at room temperature
- 200g Savoiardi (sponge fingers)
- 2 tbsp cocoa powder, for dusting
- 25g dark chocolate, finely grated (optional)

## Method

1. Mix the cooled coffee with the liqueur, if using, in a shallow dish.
2. Whisk the egg yolks with the sugar for 3–4 minutes until pale and thick.
3. Add the mascarpone and beat gently until smooth.
4. In a clean bowl, whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then fold them into
   the mascarpone mixture in three batches, keeping as much air in as
   possible.
5. Dip each sponge finger briefly into the coffee — a second or two per
   side, so they soften without going soggy — and lay them in a single
   layer in a 20 x 30cm dish.
6. Spread half the mascarpone cream over the sponge fingers.
7. Repeat with another layer of dipped sponge fingers and the rest of the
   cream. Smooth the top.
8. Cover and chill for at least 6 hours, ideally overnight.
9. Just before serving, dust generously with cocoa through a sieve and
   scatter over the grated chocolate.

## Notes

- The recipe uses raw eggs. Use pasteurised eggs if serving to anyone
  pregnant, elderly, very young or with a weakened immune system.
- For an alcohol-free version, leave out the liqueur and add 1 tsp vanilla
  extract to the coffee.
- Keeps, covered, in the fridge for up to 2 days.
