# Acme Robotics (ACMR) — Q3 Earnings Note

**Analyst:** Equity Research Desk · **Rating:** Hold · **Price target:** $64

## Summary
Acme Robotics reported Q3 revenue of $1.42bn (+9% YoY), in line with consensus.
Gross margin expanded 120bp to 41.3% on a richer mix of warehouse automation
systems. Operating expenses rose faster than revenue as the company kept
investing in its service network.

- Order backlog of $3.1bn gives roughly 2 years of revenue visibility.
- Free cash flow was $180m, helped by better working-capital management.
- Management reiterated full-year guidance of $5.6–5.8bn in revenue.
- Exposure to a slowdown in e-commerce capex remains the main risk.

risk and reward at current levels and keep our Hold rating.
